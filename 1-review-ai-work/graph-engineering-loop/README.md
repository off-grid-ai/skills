# Make a few calls, and have the rule applied everywhere
![](overview.png)

Decide a few calls on a change review page. Your agent works out the rule behind them, writes it down, applies it to every app the change touches, shows you the result before and after, and asks you less on the next change.

## Use it when
- You picked options on the calls of a change review page.
- You left notes on changed screens and want them carried everywhere.
- You want a change review to keep improving on a schedule until you stop it.

Try it: "I've decided the calls on the dark mode change review. Run the graph-engineering-loop."

Works on pages made by [graph-engineering](../graph-engineering).
