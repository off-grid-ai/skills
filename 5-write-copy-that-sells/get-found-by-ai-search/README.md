# Get your articles found and quoted by search and AI answers

![](overview.png)

Your agent plans and writes articles that answer the question a reader would actually type, with the answer up top, facts checked against the source, and one clear next step. The same structure helps Google and AI answer engines understand and quote the page.

Use it when:
- You want a list of article titles people would search for.
- You're writing or revising a how-to, a "best X" list or a README meant to bring in users.
- You want to know which of your existing articles work and why, without guessing.

Try it: "/get-found-by-ai-search Plan ten article titles for our app from the last three releases."
