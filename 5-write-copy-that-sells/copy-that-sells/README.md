# Get copy that sells, not copy that just sounds nice

![](overview.png)

Your agent writes headlines, landing pages and CTAs with the direct-response rules of Robert Bly and David Ogilvy: research first, benefits over features, the Four U's for headlines, and real proof behind every claim.

Use it when:
- A headline or hero section isn't pulling its weight.
- You're writing a landing page, ad, sales email or store listing.
- Copy lists features and you need it to say what the reader gets.

Try it: "/copy-that-sells Give me five headlines for this landing page and score each on the Four U's."
