# Search evidence and reference selection

Reviewed for this workflow on 2026-09-29. Recheck time-sensitive search guidance before using it as current advice.

## Sources of authority

1. Current primary product documentation and implementation support product claims.
2. Official search documentation supports statements about that search service.
3. The author's analytics support only the metrics actually collected.
4. Community skills offer editorial heuristics, not access to ranking systems or guarantees.

Google's AI search guidance says core SEO practices apply and no special AI markup is required:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Google's updates record FAQ rich-result retirement in May 2026:
https://developers.google.com/search/updates

Helpful-content guidance:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

Keep useful visible FAQs. Do not promise FAQ rich results or require JSON-LD in a hosted article. DEV controls its site templates, robots directives, and sitemap. An article edit does not grant control of that infrastructure.

## Synthesis of supplied sources

- The user's copywriting rulebook: reader research, specific benefits, authentic proof, short clear prose, and a concrete next step. Do not import fabricated sales results, mandatory urgency, guarantees, or an arbitrary opening sentence length.
- SNLabat SEO-GEO-AEO-Skill: inspect real page evidence, direct answers, meaningful headings, comparisons, and source attribution. Its full-site audit procedure is a separate workflow, not required for an article revision.
- AgriciDaniel claude-seo: usefulness, clear authorship, verifiable evidence, primary-source guidance, and a distinction between heuristic scores and measured performance. Treat statistics and fixed content-length suggestions as claims to verify, not rules.
- mykpono ultimate-seo-geo and GRCEngClub seo-skills: intent coverage, clear page ownership, related content links, concise answers, and no duplicate guides aimed at the same task.
- Auriti-Labs geo-optimizer-skill and Canonry: distinguish readiness checks from actual citation observations and referral traffic. Do not install trackers or incur API costs as part of ordinary writing.
- discoveredlabs awesome-aeo-seo-tools: discovery directory, not proof that a tactic works.
- HubSpot's AEO Fundamentals public course overview: trust, search intent, helpful page structure, and honest measurement. Reading the overview is not completion of its lessons.

## Measurement

Report views, clicks, impressions, engagement, and citations as distinct metrics. DEV lifetime views alone do not identify search traffic, search position, AI citations, or AI share of voice. Compare article age and exposure where data permits.

If citation tracking is requested, define a fixed query set, engine, date, locale, and repeated-run method. Record cited URLs and supporting responses. One response is an observation, not a stable visibility score.

Do not infer engine performance from word counts, llms.txt, keyword density, or a proprietary audit score. Do not claim a structure caused higher views without suitable evidence.
