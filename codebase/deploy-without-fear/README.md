# Deploy on any day without holding your breath

Before a production deploy, your agent checks CI, env vars, migrations and the rollback plan. After it, it checks health, the flows that make you money, errors and latency, and tells you to roll back the moment the numbers say so.

Use it when:
- You're about to deploy to production.
- You just deployed and want to know it's really fine.
- Your team has had one too many 2am rollbacks.

Try it: "/deploy-without-fear we're shipping v2.3 to prod"
